Executive Summary & Background

In recent times, Kaduna has witnessed a surge in data talent training through local tech hubs, online bootcamps, and government-backed initiatives like the 3MTT program. However, a significant gap remains: many learners struggle to translate theoretical knowledge into actionable, real-world business insights and rarely get platforms to showcase their capabilities.

To bridge this gap and officially herald the upcoming KaDAI Summit, we are hosting a high-impact, 1-day practical mini-hackathon on Saturday, August 29, 2026. Designed for data analysts and AI enthusiasts alike, this event emphasizes project-based learning, problem-solving, and direct exposure to industry experts and regional policymakers.

2

Event Objectives

Promote Project-Based Learning

Move learners away from passive video tutorials into active, hands-on real-world problem solving.

Bridge Skills-to-Insight Gap

Test participants' ability to turn raw datasets into compelling business stories and functional ML models.

Talent Discovery

Expose top regional data and AI talent to potential employers, partners, and the tech ecosystem.

Community Engagement

Serve as the official pre-summit anchor event to build momentum for the upcoming KaDAI Summit.

Tracks & Deliverables

The hackathon is completely tool-agnostic. Participants work in their preferred track supported by on-site physical mentors.

Track 1

Data Analysis

The Challenge:

Solve a localized business or civic problem using datasets sourced directly from the Kaduna Bureau of Statistics (KDBS).

Deliverables:
  • Cleaned data outputs
  • Analytical findings
  • Interactive dashboard / Visual report presentation
Track 2

AI & Machine Learning

The Challenge:

Build predictive or generative solutions tackling a specific domain problem statement.

Deliverables:
  • Clean, well-annotated notebook
  • Trained Machine Learning model
  • Functional prototype / deployment (Optional)
5

AI Tool Policy

Permitted
Generative AI Policy & Guideline:

Participants are permitted to use generative AI tools (LLMs, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT) for code debugging, syntax assistance, and workflow acceleration. However, the core analytical narrative, logic, feature engineering, and live defense must be driven entirely by the participant.

Format

Time Limit
