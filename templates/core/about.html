Who We Are

MannieLytics is a premier data education and consultancy firm dedicated to bridging the gap between academic theory and industry-grade execution. Founded by Ibrahim Manasseh, we specialize in transforming aspiring data enthusiasts into high-impact Data Analysts, Data Scientists, and Data Engineers.

What We Do

Comprehensive solutions for learners and businesses.

Data Analysis

Turning raw data into strategic assets. We specialize in descriptive and inferential analysis that uncovers hidden patterns and trends. From automated reporting in Power BI to complex statistical modeling, we provide the clarity needed for evidence-based decision-making. We don’t just show you the numbers; we tell the story behind them.

ML/AI

Engineering the next generation of intelligent systems. From predictive modeling and NLP to advanced Computer Vision, we build scalable AI solutions. Our expertise lies in developing "Agentic Systems" and deep learning architectures that automate complex tasks and optimize workflows.

Data Engineering

Building the backbone of data-driven organizations. We design and manage robust data pipelines, ETL processes, and secure database architectures using SQL and Python. Our focus is on ensuring data integrity, accessibility, and high-speed processing.

EdTech

Bridging the gap between theory and industry. Through MannieLytics DSA, we develop industry-aligned curricula and hybrid learning frameworks that empower students and professionals to master high-demand tech skills through practical mentorship.

Our Origin

A Response to the "Knowledge Gap"

MannieLytics started as a response to the clear gap in the tech education space—where many were learning how to code but few were learning how to solve real business problems.

The "One Week One EDA" Challenge

Our journey began with this challenge, seeing an incredible turnout of about 200 enrollments.

Flagship Python for Data

We expanded to include specialized trainings, empowering hundreds of learners globally.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark for data education, empowering the next generation of African tech talent. We aspire to make world-class tech education entirely free in the future through strategic global partnerships.

Our Mission

To provide world-class, practical data training that evolves with the speed of AI, ensuring our students are not just employable, but indispensable.
